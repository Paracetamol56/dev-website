Print shops usually ask for a PDF with printing marks, and adding them normally means opening a layout program. This tool does it in the browser: upload a PDF, choose the marks you need, check the preview and download the result. You can add crop marks with a bleed set per side in millimetres or inches, registration marks, colour bars, and a line of page information with page numbers and your own text. The file is processed on your device and is never uploaded.
